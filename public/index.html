COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How do I join a savings circle? +

Open My Circles, choose an available circle, complete your contribution, and choose your preferred payout week once the circle becomes full.

When is my contribution considered paid? +

Your contribution is considered paid only after the payment is successfully confirmed by the backend payment process.

When can I choose my payout week? +

Payout weeks become available after the circle reaches its required number of members and the weekly payout schedule has been created.

Where can I see my payout date? +

Your scheduled payout is displayed in Overview, My Circles and Payouts.

Is my PayPal password stored by PayaCircle? +

No. Sensitive PayPal credentials are not displayed in your PayaCircle member interface.
